# Chain and token logos

All logos are shipped locally. The wallet never fetches an image over the
network.

## Sources

The chain icons (`btc.png`, `ltc.png`, `doge.png`, `eth.png`, `bnb.png`) are
from the `spothq/cryptocurrency-icons` repository, `128/color` set:

<https://github.com/spothq/cryptocurrency-icons>

License: MIT (repository license). The marks themselves are trademarks of
their respective projects. They are used to identify the supported asset, not
to imply endorsement.

Token icons already shipped for USDT, USDC, BUSD, CAKE, BTCB, XRP and ADA came
from the same local icon set.

## Rules

- Do not substitute a lookalike logo for XAUT or an unknown token.
- Unknown tokens fall back to a neutral wallet icon.
- A logo is not proof that a contract is authentic; the contract address and
  issuer page are the proof.
